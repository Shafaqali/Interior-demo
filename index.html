PHILOSOPHY & PRINCIPAL

"A home should not be decorated; it should be felt."

Founded by Aurelia Vance, our Milan & New York based studio conceives residences, private retreats, and art galleries that transcend momentary decor trends.

With training at the Politecnico di Milano and years working alongside European heritage stonemasons, we approach interior design through light sculpturing, organic textural pairings, raw travertine, and quiet bespoke craft. Every corner is carefully calibrated to bring peaceful stillness to modern life.
